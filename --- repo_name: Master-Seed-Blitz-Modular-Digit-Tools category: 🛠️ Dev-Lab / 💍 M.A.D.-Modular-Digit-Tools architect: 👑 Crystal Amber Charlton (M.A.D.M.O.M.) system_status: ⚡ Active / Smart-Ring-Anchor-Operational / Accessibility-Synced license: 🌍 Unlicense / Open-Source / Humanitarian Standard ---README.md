## 🧩 Key Protocol Modules & Emojis

### 1. ⚙️ Component Architecture & Tensioning Dynamics

* **🌴 Palm Anchor Base**: Stable foundational module securing larger cleaning rags and heavy-duty utility tools without restricting hand motion.
* **💍 Independent Digit Rings**: Modular silicone and rubber rings attaching directly to individual fingers, utilizing Velcro and rubber-band tensioning for flush tool-head seating and full dexterity.
* **🤌 The "Gesture Trigger"**: Mechanical design allowing users to bend the knuckle to deploy active tool heads (brushes, sponges) and flatten the palm to retract or hide them.

### 2. ♿ Universal Accessibility & Restorative Autonomy

* **👵 The Accessibility Protocol**: Engineered for users with arthritis, limited grip strength, or motor control challenges, providing an empowering alternative to cumbersome, sweat-prone full gloves.
* **🦶 Multi-Mount Versatility**: Scalable framework adaptable for hand, foot, or arm-mounting, enabling uncompromised creative execution (such as painting or detailing) regardless of physical range of motion.
